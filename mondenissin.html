Sales · Trade marketing · Distribution

Store-level customer analytics: every sari-sari store, not just every distributor

Combine distributor sell-out, salesman orders and modern trade POS data to predict, outlet by outlet, which stores will run out and which SKU to push next. Pick a channel and region, then click a store.

Sample, illustrative outlets, SKUs and figures only, not Monde Nissin data.

How it's built: distributor DMS feeds, salesman app orders and retailer POS data are shared into Snowflake and matched to each outlet. A model learns each store's buying rhythm (payday, weather, school calendar), so salesmen get a daily list of which stores to visit and what to offer. The same data shows plants which SKUs to make more of.

Supply chain · Planning · Logistics

Supply chain control tower: from flour silo to sari-sari shelf

One live view of days of cover at every node: raw materials, plants, DCs, distributors and stores. Pick a disruption to see where it bites, and what the system recommends. Click a node for the detail.

NodeDays of coverStatus
Select a node to see the root cause and recommended action.

Sample, illustrative nodes, scenarios and figures only, not Monde Nissin data.

How it's built: supplier ASNs, plant schedules, DC stock, distributor inventory and sell-out land in Snowflake as one model. Losses are tagged the same way at every step, like a zero-loss tree from plant to shelf, so planners see the root cause, not just the shortage. Scenario runs re-plan production, stock transfers and allocation in minutes, and AI agents draft the actions for approval.
