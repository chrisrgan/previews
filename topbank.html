Show me our customer segments, which ones are at risk of leaving, and who is ready for a cross-sell offer.

Before: Deposits, loans, cards and app data sit in separate systems, so building one customer list takes weeks of IT requests.

Now: One view of each customer. Click a segment to see who to keep and what to offer next.

4

Fraud monitoring: spot unusual transactions in time for AFASA

You ask
Show me today's unusual transactions and explain why each one was flagged.

Before: Signals sit in separate card, app and transfer systems, so suspicious activity is often found after the money has moved.

Now: Transactions, device and login data land in one place in near real time. Click an alert to see exactly why it was flagged.

5

BSP reporting: faster regulatory reports

You ask
Prepare this month's BSP report pack, reconcile it to the general ledger and list anything that needs a human check.

Before: Data is pulled from several systems into spreadsheets, reconciled by hand, then checked again before submission.

Now: Report data is prepared once from governed sources, and Cortex AI handles the manual prep and flags only the exceptions.
