1

Customer analytics: one view of every customer

The problem

Deposits, loans, cards and app activity sit in separate systems, so it is hard to see the full relationship with each customer, who is drifting away, or what they are likely to need next.

On Snowflake

Core banking, loan, digital channel and branch data come together in one governed place. Segments, churn scores and next best offers are built once and shared with marketing, branches and digital teams. With Snowflake Intelligence, teams can ask questions in plain English instead of waiting for a report.

The outcome

Clear segments, earlier warning on customers likely to leave, and better targeted cross-sell offers, without IT building a new report for every request.

2

Credit risk and collections

The problem

Late payments are often spotted only after they happen, and collections teams work from static lists.

On Snowflake

Repayment history, account behavior and channel activity feed a risk model that scores accounts daily, so collectors can focus on who to call first.

The outcome

Earlier action on accounts likely to miss payments and a cleaner loan book.

3

Fraud monitoring for AFASA

The problem

AFASA raises the bar on detecting and acting on suspicious transactions, but signals sit across channels and systems.

On Snowflake

Transactions, device and login data land in one place in near real time. Anomaly detection flags unusual activity, and investigators can review cases with full context.

The outcome

Faster detection, clearer audit trails and less manual review.

4

Faster BSP reporting

The problem

Regulatory reports often mean pulling data from many systems into spreadsheets, then checking it by hand.

On Snowflake

Report data is prepared once from governed sources. Cortex AI helps with reconciliations, checks and first drafts of commentary.

The outcome

Less manual prep, fewer errors and more time for the team to focus on analysis.
