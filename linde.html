The platform

What Snowflake does

One governed place for all your data, so analytics and AI run on the same numbers everyone trusts.

Bring it together

SAP/ERP orders, plant historians and SCADA, telemetry, fleet GPS and spreadsheets land in one place. You don't have to rip and replace anything on AWS.

Analyze at any scale

Compute that scales up for month-end and back down after. You pay only for what you use, so costs stay predictable.

AI on your data

Forecasting, anomaly detection and plain-English questions with Cortex AI. The data never leaves your governed account.

Share securely

Give regional, finance or customer teams live, governed views with no file exports and no version drift.

Python, SQL, notebooks

Data engineers and data scientists work side by side with Snowpark, notebooks and dbt, on one copy of the data.

Cylinder & distribution: fewer lost cylinders, fuller trucks

Combine tank telemetry, cylinder scans and delivery routes so you refill before customers run low.

How it's built: telemetry and scan data land in Snowflake in near real time. Consumption models project when each tank hits its reorder point, and routes are grouped by area. Idle and overdue cylinders are surfaced for rental billing or pickup.
